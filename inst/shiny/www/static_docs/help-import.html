Importing text files

DIVIANA accepts .txt, .tsv and .csv files and detects column separators automatically. The following three formats are supported:

  • Genotype table. One row per sample, with either one genotype column per marker (e.g. 12/14), or two adjacent allele columns per marker.
    Example 1 
    Sample	D8S1179	TPOX
R1	12/14	8/11
R2	13/13	9/11
    Example 2 
    Sample	D8S1179.1	D8S1179.2	TPOX.1	TPOX.2
R1	12	14	8	11
R2	13	13	9	11
  • Genotype table with relationship information. As above, but specifying each reference's relationship to the missing person for automatic pedigree construction. The first three columns are Family, Relationship (or Kinship) and Sample, in that order; minor naming variations are accepted. Relationships must be in brackets, e.g. [Mother].
    Example 
    Family	Relationship	Sample	D8S1179	TPOX
F1	[Mother]	R1	12/14	8/11
F1	[Father]	R2	13/13	9/11
F2	[Brother]	R3	12/12	8/8
  • GeneMapper. DIVIANA supports some exports from GeneMapper software (Thermo Fisher Scientific). In the GeneMapper export tool, choose "One line per marker" and ensure the file contains columns Sample Name, Marker, Allele 1 and Allele 2. Special samples (blanks, controls and ladders) are automatically detected by DIVIANA and hidden by default.
    Example 
    Sample Name	Marker	Allele 1	Allele 2
R1	D8S1179	12	14
R1	TPOX	8	11
R2	D8S1179	13	13
R2	TPOX	9	11
